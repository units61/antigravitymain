## 3. WebGL & Shader Physics Guidelines

1. **Camera Path Choreography**:
   - The scroll trigger range must span sequentially from `0.0` (top of the page) to `1.0` (bottom of the page).
   - Coordinate the camera path dynamically:
     - For *Luxury*: Slower, cinematic tracks like slow pan (`pan`) or orbital sweep (`orbit`).
     - For *Cyberpunk*: Sudden snaps, close zoom sweeps (`zoom`), or rapid camera dive tracks.

2. **Custom Shader Aesthetics**:
   - **glass**: Perfect for luxury/minimalist interfaces. Configure high glass refraction (`refraction: true`) and chromatic aberration (`0.02` to `0.08`) to give a realistic lens refraction.
   - **liquid-flow**: Ideal for organic/calm themes. Apply continuous vertex shader wave deformations (`liquid_movement: true`) simulating fluid water or botanical breathing motions.
   - **neon-glitch**: Best for rebel/cyberpunk. Apply RGB-shifting fragment shaders.

3. **Experience Pattern Library (Narrative WebGL Presets)**:
   - To maintain Awwwards-tier quality, select and map your `scene_choreography` elements and materials from this curated library of proven spatial pattern presets:
     - `Camera Flythrough`: Map camera path steps directly through the geometric center of hollow 3D primitives or meshes, creating an immersive flight trajectory.
     - `Product Explosion`: Disassemble a multi-part 3D model by dynamically multiplying the mesh positions based on scroll depth triggers (e.g., expanding position vectors symmetrically on scroll).
     - `Particle Reveal`: Generate floating particles that drift and coalesce into a solid typographic mesh structure as scroll progress enters the threshold.
     - `Liquid Morph`: Deploy custom liquid shaders featuring sine wave vertex deformations and slow perlin noise displacements, creating organic, living liquid shapes.
     - `Typography Collision`: Align massive titles on opposing sides of the viewport, merging them smoothly onto the center axis as the camera sweep snaps to a stop.
     - `Infinite Gallery`: Lay out mesh image cards on a cylindrical or spiral 3D camera trajectory, enabling users to fly down an endless portal of showcases.

4. **Lighting & Atmosphere Harmony**:
   - Match colors perfectly to the Brand Strategy:
     - E.g., Luxury: Dark environment with a low spotlight reflecting off chrome/gold surfaces + gold dust particles.
     - E.g., Calm/Organic: Soft studio keylights, bright white fog (`fog_color: "#FFFDF9"`), and floating dust stars.
